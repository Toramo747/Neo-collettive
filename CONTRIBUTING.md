# Contributing

External contributions to Neo-collettive (NEO / MYCELIX) require acceptance of a Contributor License Agreement (CLA) before they can be merged.

The CLA must grant Andrea Gava the right to use, modify, distribute, sublicense, and re-license the contribution under this project license or another license. Contributors retain ownership of their contributions, subject to the rights granted by the CLA.

Do not submit secrets, credentials, personal data, or third-party code that you do not have the right to contribute.
