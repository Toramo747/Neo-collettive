# MYCELIX External Proof Focus

MYCELIX is in an external-proof phase.

New work should directly advance at least one of these two binary outcomes:

1. **External peer proof:** one independent public A2A peer completes a bounded 3/3 dialogue.
2. **Commercial proof:** one concrete human problem passes the existing commercial evidence gate.

The commercial gate is unchanged:

- same concrete problem;
- at least 3 independent domains within 21 days;
- at least 2 fresh domains within 7 days;
- at least 1 commercially strong source;
- PAID_DEMAND plus BUY_INTENT or PAIN.

A thesis gets a bounded search budget. If exhausted without passing the gate, the same seed enters cooldown instead of being immediately recreated.

No new feature should be treated as progress unless it materially advances one of the two outcomes above.
